## What this index means

This is the visible, versioned baseline of research and collaboration practices currently used in this repository. It is not a catalogue of universal best practices, a certification, or evidence that an external consortium has ratified the framework.

The entries below were adopted as the project's working method by its current maintainer through D-09. They have not yet been independently reviewed or ratified by external contributors. That limitation is part of the record, not a footnote to hide.

A future change to this baseline must have a stable practice ID, a proposal and evidence record, version-specific review, a reasoned ratification decision, contributor attribution where applicable, and a traceable merge commit. Material changes require renewed review. Superseded entries remain in Git history and decision records.

## Review requirements for future changes

1. Open a proposal with a stable practice ID and version.
2. State the problem, scope, evidence, alternatives, limitations and adoption criteria.
3. Identify relevant review expertise and invite independent review.
4. Record the exact version reviewed and each material objection.
5. Obtain the applicable review and ratification required by the governance framework.
6. Confirm contributor attribution and consent; distinguish proposal, contribution, review, approval and endorsement.
7. Update this index and affected documents in a pull request.
8. Record the merge commit SHA and retain the prior version in history.

No automation currently performs this adoption process. No external ratification, peer endorsement or multi-party consortium is claimed.
